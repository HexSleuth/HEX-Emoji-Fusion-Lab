## 📖 Overview
**HEX Emoji Fusion Lab** is an interactive web-based reactor where you can combine different emojis to create totally unique emoji mixtures. Pick "Ingredient A" and "Ingredient B", fire up the reactor, and see what comes out! Track your discoveries as you play, explore custom fusions, and copy your favorites to your clipboard in a single click.
## ✨ Features
*   **⚛️ Fusion Reactor:** Combine two different emojis (Ingredient A + Ingredient B) to discover a unique result.
*   **🧪 Ingredient Lab:** A rich palette of emojis ready to be mixed. 
*   **🔍 Search HEX Emoji:** Quickly locate specific emojis to drop into your fusion slots.
*   **🏆 Discovery Tracker:** Keep track of the combinations you've unlocked (Can you find all 120 base discoveries?).
*   **➕ Custom Inputs:** Add any custom emoji to the mix and see if it creates a wildcard fusion.
*   **📜 Fusion History:** A running log of your session's previous fusions so you never lose a cool combo.
*   **📋 Click-to-Copy:** Easily copy the fused result or the fusion formula directly to your clipboard.
## ⌨️ Keyboard & Mouse Controls
Navigate the lab like a pro with these built-in shortcuts:

| Action | Control/Shortcut | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Add Ingredient** | `Tap / Click` | Tap an emoji in the lab to add it to slot A or B. |
| **Copy Result** | `Tap / Click` | Tap a fused emoji to instantly copy it to your clipboard. |
| **Copy Formula** | `Hold Click` | Click and hold on a fusion to copy the entire math formula (A + B = C). |
| **Fuse!** | `Enter` | Triggers the fusion reactor. |
| **Randomize** | `R` | Loads random ingredients into the reactor. |
| **Clear Slots** | `Esc` | Clears the current ingredients from the reactor. |

## 🏗️ Lab Sections
1. **Fusion Reactor:** The main mixing board. Watch your emojis merge.
2. **Ingredient Palette:** Select your base elements from the grid.
3. **Browse Combos:** Lock an emoji into Slot A and browse its possible known combinations.
4. **Discoveries:** A collection board that tracks your progress out of the 120 known stable fusions.
5. **History & Custom Fusions:** Review your past experiments and custom wildcard mixtures during your session.
