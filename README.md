Welcome to the **HEX Emoji Fusion Lab × Kitchen**—an advanced, highly immersive web experiment that interfaces with the **Google Emoji Kitchen** ecosystem. Mix, match, search, and discover hundreds of unique custom emoji mashups right in your browser with zero bloat!

🌐 **[Experience the Live Demo Here](https://hexsleuth.github.io/HEX-Emoji-Fusion-Lab/)**

---
## ✨ Features
- **🧬 Emoji Fusion Reactor**: Combine *Ingredient A* and *Ingredient B* to reveal official Emoji Kitchen creations.
- **📋 One-Tap Copy**: Instantly copy any emoji or fusion directly to your clipboard just by tapping it.
- **🔍 Kitchen Search**: Easily search through the extensive library of supported Emoji Kitchen combinations.
- **➕ Custom Ingredients**: Want to go off-script? Add any custom emoji to the palette and experiment.
- **🏆 Discovery Tracker**: Hunt down specific fusions! Track your progress as you collect predefined discoveries (e.g., *0 / 120 discovered*).
- **🕰️ Session History**: Never lose a great combination. Your recent fusions and custom creations are logged in the History panel.
- **⚡ Hotkey Support**: Quickly fuse, copy, or randomize using keyboard shortcuts.
---
## 🎮 How to Use (Controls & Shortcuts)
The lab is designed to be fully interactive and lightning-fast. Use your mouse, touch, or keyboard to cook up new emojis:

| Action | Control / Shortcut |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Select Ingredient** | Click/Tap on any emoji in the palette |
| **Copy Emoji** | **Tap/Click** any result or palette emoji |
| **Copy Formula** | **Hold** click on an emoji |
| **Fuse** | Press **`Enter`** |
| **Randomize** | Press **`R`** to pick two random ingredients |
| **Clear Lab** | Press **`Esc`** to reset the reactor |

---
## 🖥️ UI Overview
1. **Fusion Reactor**: The main stage. Slot your two emojis here and watch the fusion load.
2. **Ingredient Palette**: Your base library of emojis ready for mixing.
3. **Kitchen Search & Combos**: Find out what mixes well with your currently selected emoji.
4. **Add Any Emoji**: Input your own custom emojis to test undocumented fusions.
5. **Discoveries & History**: Keep track of what you've found and re-fuse old favorites with a single tap.
